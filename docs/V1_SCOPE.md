# Adventurer's Rise — V1 Launch Scope

**Status:** Agreed V1 boundary. Exact balance/content counts may still move during playtesting.

The goal of V1 is to prove the complete retention/progression loop while staying small enough to build primarily through Astra and Roblox Studio MCP.

---

## Must-Have V1 Systems

### Character progression

- One persistent character.
- No permanent class lock.
- Starter weapon choice: Sword, Bow, or Staff.
- 9 skills:
  - Melee
  - Defense
  - Ranged
  - Magic
  - Mining
  - Woodcutting
  - Smithing
  - Fletching
  - Arcana
- Skill cap: 60.
- Lightweight mastery tree for every skill.
- 6 permanent Ascensions with no skill reset:
  - Adventurer (starting state)
  - Adept
  - Vanguard
  - Champion
  - Runebound
  - Paragon
  - Ascendant

### World

- Central classic-medieval hub/town.
- Visible personal plots in the shared server.
- 3 compact progression regions:
  - The Borderlands
  - The Elderwood
  - The Shattered Reach
- Soft-gated world: players can enter dangerous regions early.
- Short travel times.
- Grounded medieval-adventure presentation that escalates into high fantasy.

### Standard enemies

Launch target: **12 archetypes**.

Region I — The Borderlands:
- Wolves
- Bandits
- Goblins
- Hobgoblins

Region II — The Elderwood:
- Dire Wolves
- Orc Raiders
- Forest Trolls
- Lesser Elementals

Region III — The Shattered Reach:
- Armored Orc Champions
- Arcane Constructs
- Corrupted Knights
- Greater Elementals

Enemy levels roll randomly within archetype-specific ranges. Enemies do not scale to player level.

### Bosses

Launch target: **6 shared-server bosses**.

1. Goblin Chieftain
2. Bandit Warlord
3. Troll King
4. Runic Guardian
5. Fallen Champion
6. Arcane Colossus

Boss rules:
- short respawns / continuously farmable,
- shared server encounters,
- independent personal RNG loot roll for each eligible participant,
- meaningful participation threshold,
- readable mechanics rather than raid-level complexity,
- repeatable sources of XP, Gold/materials, rare equipment, curated sets, and prestige rewards.

### Combat

- Mostly automatic soft targeting.
- Optional manual target override.
- Hold Attack for continuous attacks at weapon attack rate.
- Assisted Ranged/Magic projectiles or effects.
- No precision third-person aiming requirement.
- Conditional enemy aggression.
- Death causes no item/material/Gold loss; player only loses the fight/time and respawns safely.
- Defense XP comes from direct station training plus passive XP from meaningful combat, scaled to enemy difficulty.
- No complex ability/hotbar system at V1.

### Gathering

- Mining and Woodcutting.
- Persistent/non-depleting simulator-style nodes.
- Skill + tool progression.
- Better tools improve efficiency.
- No durability.

### Production

- Smithing, Fletching, Arcana.
- Recipe access primarily gated by skill level + materials.
- One meaningful intermediate production stage for most chains.
- Basic shared production stations in the hub.
- Personal production stations become unlockable later for player plots.
- Personal production-station upgrades primarily improve speed/convenience, not recipe access.

### Equipment

- Early game is intentionally linear.
- Crafted equipment provides dependable tier progression.
- Shops provide an alternate/convenience acquisition path.
- Bosses provide RNG alternatives, unique weapons, curated sets, and later build-defining equipment.
- Mid/mid-late game introduces set bonuses and meaningful gear combinations.
- No randomized affix loot at V1.
- Normal tier progression aggressively reuses equipment models with texture/material/paint/glow/shimmer/effect changes.
- Unique meshes reserved mainly for boss uniques/prestige items.

### Player plots

Starting functionality:
- Melee training station
- Defense training station
- Ranged training station
- Magic training station

Progression:
- training stations are upgradeable,
- upgrades improve active/offline training and visual prestige,
- later ability to construct personal Smithing/Fletching/Arcana stations,
- no freeform housing/building system.

### Offline progression

- Direct offline combat-skill training through plot combat stations.
- Offline gains are capped.
- Active play remains faster/more rewarding.
- Exact offline duration/rates remain TBD.
- No agreed V1 offline boss loot, gathering, or full economic automation.

### Economy

- One soft currency: **Gold Coins**.
- Gold earned through combat, bosses, NPC sales, and simple progression rewards.
- Players can sell gathered resources and crafted items to NPCs.
- Gold used for station construction/upgrades, shop materials/gear, mastery respecs, and other progression sinks.
- NPC pricing must prevent buy-inputs → craft → resell infinite-profit loops.
- Player trading is not V1.

### Social

- Visible plots.
- Visible Ascension status/titles.
- Recognizable equipment progression.
- Shared bosses.
- Leaderboards.
- Boss contribution/damage display.
- Rare visible boss/prestige gear.

## First-Session Retention Targets

### First 30 seconds

- Spawn at personal plot.
- Choose Sword, Bow, or Staff.
- Begin earning XP immediately.

### First ~5 minutes

Player should experience:
- combat training,
- Defense training,
- first roaming fights,
- Gold,
- basic drops.

### ~5–20 minutes

Player should discover:
- Mining,
- Woodcutting,
- shared production stations,
- shops,
- early gear progression,
- first shared boss.

### ~15–25 minutes

Target: complete **Ascension I — Adept**.

### First 1–2 hours

Target state:
- roughly **Ascension II — Vanguard**,
- Ascension III visibly within reach,
- at least one plot station upgrade,
- exposure to multiple skills,
- at least one desirable boss/item target,
- reason to return for offline gains and continued progression.

### Full launch progression target

Target roughly **20–30 hours of meaningful active progression** to reach/complete the highest launch Ascension for a normal engaged player, subject to playtesting.

This should come from many concurrent goals rather than artificial single-task walls.

| Ascension | Working combat target | Cumulative active-time target |
|---|---:|---:|
| I — Adept | Offense ~8, Defense ~5 | 15–25 min |
| II — Vanguard | Offense ~15, Defense ~10 | 45–75 min |
| III — Champion | Offense ~25, Defense ~18 | 2–3.5 hr |
| IV — Runebound | Offense ~35, Defense ~27 | 5–7 hr |
| V — Paragon | Offense ~45, Defense ~36 | 10–15 hr |
| VI — Ascendant | Offense ~55, Defense ~45 | 20–30 hr |

Every Ascension also includes overall-skilling and boss milestones. Exact skilling totals remain TBD.

## Explicitly Deferred From V1

- PvP
- tournaments
- player trading
- guilds
- pets/companions
- substantial quest chains
- stealing/risk systems
- dungeons
- raids
- freeform housing
- permanent classes
- randomized equipment affixes
- complex combat hotbars/ability systems
- crafting minigames
- consumable arrows/runes for every basic attack
- tool/equipment durability
- multiple soft currencies
- traditional limited backpack/bank loops

These are not rejected forever. They require a demonstrated reason after the core loop is proven.

---

## V1 Success Criteria

The launch design should be considered directionally successful if playtesting shows that players:

1. Understand the basic loop within seconds/minutes without a long tutorial.
2. Reach meaningful visible progress quickly.
3. Continue finding obvious goals after the first Ascension.
4. Use more than one progression system naturally over a session.
5. See stronger players/plots/equipment and understand them as aspirational rather than unreachable.
6. Find both active combat and low-attention training/gathering worthwhile.
7. Want to return after leaving because offline training and unfinished goals matter.
8. Can make continuous progress without encountering a deliberate paywall or miserable grind spike.

Implementation should remain small, reusable, reversible, and playtest-driven.
